Data stewardship

A durable public record

The portfolio is mirrored from its source workbook and converted into versioned, inspectable JSON. The website adds presentation without changing the underlying records.

  • Open formats ODS and JSON remain readable without a proprietary platform.
  • Traceable sources Every dataset records its workbook, worksheet, and source digest.
  • Simple delivery Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript work on any ordinary static host.

Download the ODS mirror or review the data specifications.
